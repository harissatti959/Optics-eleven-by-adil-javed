Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I undergo a comprehensive eye exam?

We recommend an annual comprehensive eye exam for adults and school-age children. Screen intensive workers and contact lens wearers should visit every 6 to 12 months.

Are the designer frames at Optics Eleven guaranteed authentic?

Yes, 100%. All frames come with genuine manufacturer warranty, certificate of authenticity, serial number verification, and brand cases directly from official international distributors (Tom Ford, Lindberg, Gucci, Oliver Peoples, Ray-Ban).

Do I need an appointment or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome for frame browsing, adjustments, and boutique inquiries. However, for specialized clinical consultations and refraction with Dr. Adil Javed, prior appointment booking is strongly advised to prevent waiting.

Can you customize lenses for high prescriptions or astigmatism?

Yes. We specialize in ultra-high index 1.74 lenses, customized free-form digital progressives, blue-light defense, and chromatic aberration reduction tailored to sub-millimeter pupillary distance.
